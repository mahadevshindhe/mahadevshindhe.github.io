Week 1• Theme: AI Foundations for Solution Architects

Overview

This week gives you a systems-level view of Artificial Intelligence: definitions, the end-to-end lifecycle, common architecture patterns, integration approaches, and governance considerations. Each day has hands-on exercises and an artifact to produce. By week-end you'll have an architect-ready 3–5 page deliverable that becomes your foundation for Week 2.

Day 1 — AI Fundamentals (Architect's Perspective)

Goal: Understand AI as an architectural capability

Topics

  • AI, ML, DL definitions (architect-focused)
  • AI vs deterministic software
  • Predictive vs Generative AI
  • AI as a system component

Hands-on

  • Write a 1-paragraph: “What is AI from an architect’s lens?”
  • List 5 AI use cases in your domain
Deliverable: 1-page summary — “AI as an architectural capability in my enterprise landscape.”

Day 2 — AI System Lifecycle (End-to-End)

Goal: Learn phases of building & operating AI systems

Topics

  • Data collection → preparation → training → validation → deployment → monitoring
  • Feedback loops, model decay & concept drift
  • Offline pipelines vs online inference
  • Batch vs real-time workloads

Hands-on

  • Draw an AI lifecycle diagram (annotate where architect decisions happen)
  • Mark infra, integration, and data quality decision points
Deliverable: AI Lifecycle Diagram (architect version)

Day 3 — AI Architecture Patterns

Goal: Learn common patterns used in modern AI systems

Topics

  • 3-layer AI architecture: Data → Model → App
  • Online vs Offline architectures
  • Centralized vs Decentralized model serving
  • Hybrid (rules + AI) architectures
  • Inference patterns: sync / async / batch / streaming

Hands-on

  • Draw a high-level architecture for one use case: fraud detection, recommender, or chat assistant
Deliverable: High-level AI Architecture Diagram

Day 4 — AI in Enterprise Architecture & System Integration

Goal: Learn how AI plugs into existing landscapes

Topics

  • AI as microservice / API-based inference
  • Managed services vs custom stacks
  • Event-driven systems, lakehouses, orchestration
  • Latency, cost, throughput, GPU constraints

Hands-on

  • Pick a live system and sketch "before" and "after" AI integration diagrams
Deliverable: Enterprise Integration Blueprint

Day 5 — AI Limitations, Risks & Governance

Goal: Identify risks and architect governance controls

Topics

  • Hallucinations, bias, reliability, explainability
  • Responsible AI principles and policies
  • Security: prompt injection, data leakage
  • When NOT to use AI

Hands-on

  • Create a checklist: "Is AI the right solution?"
  • Build a risk register for one chosen use case
Deliverable: AI Risk & Governance Checklist + Do/Don't Decision Matrix

  • Microsoft: Responsible AI Standard (overview)
  • AWS: ML Lens (architecture guide)

Mini-Project (Week-long)

Combine your artifacts into a single 3–5 page document that becomes your Week 1 deliverable.

Mini-Project Checklist

  • One-page: AI in my enterprise landscape
  • AI lifecycle diagram (annotated)
  • High-level architecture examples
  • Before/After integration blueprint
  • Risk & governance checklist

Presentation

  • Prepare a 10–12 slide deck summarizing the deliverable
  • Keep visuals simple: diagrams, bullets, decision matrix

Final Deliverable (End of Week)

A consolidated 3–5 page architect-ready document + a short presentation. Use the deliverable as your Week 2 kickoff artifact.

Deliverable structure (expand)
  1. Executive summary: what AI capability you propose
  2. AI lifecycle diagram with decision points
  3. Architecture diagrams (2 examples)
  4. Integration blueprint & system impact
  5. Risk register, governance checklist & Do/Don't matrix
  6. Next steps & recommended Week 2 focus
